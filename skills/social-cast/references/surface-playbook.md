# Surface Playbook (Social)

Rank platforms:

| Platform | Audience fit /5 | Format fit /5 | Capacity cost /5 (lower=easier) | Priority |
|----------|----------------:|--------------:|--------------------------------:|----------|

Rules:

- Score capacity honestly (video-heavy = high cost)
- Drop surfaces scoring fit <3 unless strategic bet
- One “home” platform + ≤2 satellites for most teams
